# Booking La Rosa View

## How to book
Send a booking request with the form at `/booking` (the "Book La Rosa" and "Request a Quote" buttons lead there).
The form asks for:
- full name (required),
- email (required),
- phone number (optional),
- event type (required): Private Party, Wedding, Corporate Event, Festival, Club Night or Other,
- event date (required),
- location, city or venue (required),
- additional details: for example the expected number of guests and special requirements.

## What happens after a request
A request is not a booking. The team reviews it and replies directly, typically within 24 hours ("We'll review and
respond within 24h"), to confirm or decline and to work out the details. The general rule for every brand is in
[[Booking requests]].

## Availability
> [!incomplete] There is no public calendar and no live availability. Only the team can say whether a date is free, after a request.

## Prices and quotes
> [!incomplete] No price, fee or package is published. A quote is given by the team in reply to a booking request.

## Deposits, payment, cancellation
> [!incomplete] No deposit, payment or cancellation terms are published for La Rosa View; they are agreed directly with the team ([[Booking requests]]).
