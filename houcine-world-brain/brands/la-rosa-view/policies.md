# La Rosa View policies

## Booking requests are not confirmations
A request sent through the site is a request, not a confirmed booking: see [[Booking requests]]. A booking is
confirmed only when the La Rosa View team confirms it directly.

## Payment, deposits, cancellation
> [!incomplete] La Rosa View publishes no payment method, currency, deposit, cancellation or rescheduling policy. These are agreed directly with the team once it replies to a request. (The EUR/USD and payment-method terms on the site belong to [[Houcine.tech]] and [[Houcine.studio]], not to La Rosa View.)

## Reviews
Visitors can leave a review at `/review` (name, optional email, a rating and a text). Submitting it sends the review
to the team; the site does not publish submitted reviews automatically, so no one should be told their review will
appear on the site.
