# DJ Hajri Mohamed (DJ La Rosa)

## Role
Hajri Mohamed is the DJ and music producer at [[La Rosa View]], performing as DJ La Rosa.

## Experience and style
- More than 2 years of experience ("+2 Years of Experience").
- More than 50 events played, "from private parties to massive clubs".
- Versatile sets: House, Techno, Afrobeat and more.
- Performs across Tunisia ("spreading music across the nation").

## Background
He started young: the site says he was already creating, producing and performing at 15 and 16, and the brand's
story mentions "mixing at 15". His story is described as "about dreams, creativity, and passion — the belief that
with effort and heart, you can turn vision into reality."

## Media
He was featured in an interview on Al Hayet FM ([[La Rosa View events and releases]]).
