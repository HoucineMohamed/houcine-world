# La Rosa View questions and answers

These are short answers that point to the notes holding the facts; the concierge composes its own replies from the
notes and does not recite this list.

## Does La Rosa View play weddings?
Yes, "Wedding" is one of the event types of the booking form. See [[Booking La Rosa View]].

## What music does DJ La Rosa play?
House, Techno, Afrobeat and more: see [[DJ Hajri Mohamed]].

## How fast does the team answer?
Typically within 24 hours of a booking request: see [[Booking La Rosa View]].

## Is my date confirmed once I send the form?
No. A request is not a booking until the team confirms it: see [[Booking requests]].

## How much does it cost?
No price is published; the team gives a quote in reply to a request: see [[Booking La Rosa View]].

## What is N30?
The annual signature mix, renewed every November 30: see [[La Rosa View events and releases]].
