# La Rosa View events and releases

## N30, the annual signature mix
N30 is La Rosa View's annual signature mix: "One date. One mix. One vision." Every November 30 the old N30 disappears
and a new one takes its place. The current N30 can be listened to from the La Rosa View page (it links to YouTube).

## Featured on Al Hayet FM
La Rosa View was featured on Al Hayet FM: an exclusive behind-the-scenes interview ("La Rosa View X Al Hayet FM"),
watchable on YouTube from the La Rosa View and DJ pages.

## Mixes
The latest mixes are on SoundCloud ("Latest Drops"); the La Rosa View page shows releases such as "TIMBRE" and
"The View 0.1". Links: [[Contacting La Rosa View]].
