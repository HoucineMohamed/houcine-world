# La Rosa View

## What La Rosa View is
La Rosa View is a DJ and music production brand built around [[DJ Hajri Mohamed]], known as DJ La Rosa. Its tagline
is "Where Sound Meets Vision." The brand covers music production, live performances, media and visuals, and artist
collaboration (details in [[La Rosa View services]]).

## Where it is based
La Rosa View is based in Tunisia. No city, venue or street address is published.

> [!incomplete] Which cities or countries La Rosa View travels to for events is not published; see [[Booking La Rosa View]] for how to ask.

## Who is behind it
- [[DJ Hajri Mohamed]]: DJ and producer.
- [[Houcine Mohamed]]: Director.

La Rosa View is managed by [[Houcine.management]], part of the [[Houcine.world]] ecosystem
(see [[How the Houcine.world brands connect]]).
