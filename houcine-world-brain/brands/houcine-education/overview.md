# Houcine.education

## What it is
Houcine.education is a curated digital bookstore for learners exploring tech, design and innovation, and an
educational service that is still in development: it is not open yet. Its page, `/reads`, says it is under
construction.

## Buying and launch date
There is nothing to browse or buy yet: no courses, enrollment or purchases are available while the service is being
built. No launch date is set.
