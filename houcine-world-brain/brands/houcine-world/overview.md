# Houcine.world

## What it is
Houcine.world is a modular digital ecosystem of four verticals: tech, studio, education and management. They run
independently while sharing a technical base, a design language and a growth infrastructure. It is founder-built,
by Mohamed Houcine.

## The four verticals
- [[Houcine.tech]]: technology, engineering, digital systems and AI integration.
- [[Houcine.studio]]: branding, graphic design and digital experiences.
- [[Houcine.education]]: an educational service, currently under construction.
- [[Houcine.management]]: management and coordination of brands, artists, creative ventures and businesses, such as [[La Rosa View]].

## New brands
The ecosystem is designed so that new brands can be added without rebuilding the core.
