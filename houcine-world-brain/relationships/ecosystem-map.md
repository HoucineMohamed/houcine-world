# How the Houcine.world brands connect

## Structure
- [[Houcine.world]] is the ecosystem; it has four verticals: [[Houcine.tech]], [[Houcine.studio]], [[Houcine.education]] and [[Houcine.management]].
- [[Houcine.management]] manages brands; the one brand it currently manages is [[La Rosa View]], a DJ and music production brand.
- [[La Rosa View]]: [[DJ Hajri Mohamed]] is its DJ and producer; [[Houcine Mohamed]] is its Director.

## What this means for a visitor
Questions about La Rosa View go to the La Rosa View team ([[Contacting La Rosa View]]). Questions about websites,
apps, design or the other verticals belong to those brands, each with its own page and email
([[Contacting Houcine.world]]).
