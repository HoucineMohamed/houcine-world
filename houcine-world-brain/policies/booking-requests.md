# Booking requests (from the Terms, section 2)

## A request is not a booking
Submitting a booking or contact request through the site is a request, not a confirmed booking. It does not
guarantee availability, pricing, or that the brand will accept it. A member of that brand's team contacts the person
directly, using the details given, to confirm or decline the request and work out further arrangements.

## Cancellation, rescheduling, deposits and payment
These are arranged directly between the person and the brand they are booking, not through the site. The site does
not enforce or automate a cancellation or rescheduling policy; any such terms are whatever the person and the brand
agree separately.

## Availability on the site
The Terms say the site does not guarantee that booking availability shown anywhere will always be accurate.
